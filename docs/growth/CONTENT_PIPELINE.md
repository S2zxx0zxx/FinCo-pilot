# Content and authority pipeline - drafts only

## Product proof before copy

Source App.tsx includes accounts, transactions/import, budgets, goals, reports and spending-plan routes. Route presence is not successful product acceptance. Existing landing screenshots are explicitly synthetic. Verify actual journeys in an isolated environment before claiming feature availability, bank connectivity, AI capability or Safe-to-Spend accuracy.

Market default: English/global discoverability; evaluate India-specific intent only where actual product/currency/geographic suitability is proven. No unsupported UPI, bank-sync, tax/regulatory or financial-advice claims. No localization/hreflang without full reviewed variants.

## Initial research queue

| Intent seed (no volume claim) | Proposed output | Required proof |
|---|---|---|
| FinCo-Pilot / pricing / security | Verified product/entity overview | Active plans, supported features, actual contact and security evidence |
| Budgeting without bank linking / CSV import | Manual-first onboarding guide | Working manual/import journey and limitations |
| Organize transactions / budget setup | Original synthetic-data walkthrough | Actual categorization/budget behavior |
| Reports / balances / safe-to-spend | Feature explanation with assumptions | Shipped calculations, provenance and guardrails |
| Pre-signup questions | Concise contextual answers | Real question sources and product-backed answers |

Research Monarch, YNAB, PocketGuard and Actual Budget using public official pages; identify suitable India-native examples rather than assuming equivalence. Record URL/date/statement and original gap analysis. No competitor UI/copy cloning, gated scraping or invented volume/difficulty.

Score 0-5 with evidence: intent fit 25%, product proof 25%, ability to compete 20%, business value 20%, originality 10%; apply unsupported-claim/locality/finance-risk penalties. Select 5-10 proved clusters, one canonical intent owner per cluster, before creating pages.

Every brief contains audience/intent/region, query-to-URL ownership, product/version evidence, original contribution, primary sources, claim review, title/H1/description/alt/links, approved CTA, human editorial pass, publication QA and refresh date. Finance information needs dated assumptions and qualified review where legally sensitive; no fabricated author credentials.

## Brand and AI discovery

Use FinCo-Pilot consistently. Verify owned profiles/contact/logo; prepare 1-line, 50-word and 150-word descriptions after factual inputs. Draft founder story, relevant directory shortlist and personalized pitches; never auto-send/publish, buy links, fabricate reviews or case studies. Case studies require consent and real outcomes.

Schema only when visible facts qualify: Organization/WebSite, actual breadcrumbs, accurate WebApplication, attributed Article, actual video metadata. No fake offers/ratings/SearchAction. FAQ answers do not promise FAQ rich results. Preserve accurate llms/catalog files without treating them as ranking/citation requirements.

AI prompt panel: fixed questions about manual budgeting/import and verified product tasks; record engine/model/date/region/query and actual cited URL separately from brand mention and referral visits. No automatic citation promise. Search versus training policy remains an owner decision.

Publishing states: RESEARCH -> PRODUCT VERIFIED -> DRAFT -> FACT/EDITORIAL REVIEW -> OWNER APPROVED -> PREVIEW QA -> RELEASED -> LIVE VERIFIED -> OBSERVED. Nothing in this record is approved public copy.
